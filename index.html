Find the Right Wood for Your Project

A few questions make choosing wood much easier.

Where will it be used?

Frames, furniture, roofing and decorative panels each ask different things from wood.

Indoors or outdoors?

Wood that stays dry indoors faces a harder life near rain, sun or damp walls.

How long should it last?

Tell us how many years you expect from it so we can discuss suitable choices.

Look and finish

Natural grain, polish or paint: the finish you want affects which wood makes sense.

Budget

Share a range early. It is easier to find a good option when we know the limit.

Size and quantity

Lengths, thickness and counts let us check availability and give an accurate quote.
