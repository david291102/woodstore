About Sankara Narayanna & Co Wood Store

A wood store in Kovilpatti that helps customers choose suitable wood for their homes and projects.

Our Business and Products

We are a wood store on Ilayarasanendal Road, Tippo Road, Kovilpatti. Our focus is on timber, wooden doors, windows and wood products for the home. You can browse our products or look at the gallery to get ideas.

Product ranges and stock change from time to time, so please contact us to confirm what is available for your project.

Our Approach to Wood Quality

Match wood to the job

Good quality means the right wood for the use, whether it is a door frame, a shelf or a roof support.

Look before you buy

We encourage customers to check the wood in person and ask questions about grain, finish and size.

Honest guidance

If a product is not suitable for your use, we will tell you and talk through alternatives.

No exaggerated promises

We prefer to explain what we can supply and let the wood speak for itself.

Helping Homeowners and Builders

Homeowners usually want clear advice and a fair price. Builders, contractors, carpenters and interior designers need correct sizes and reliable supply. We try to serve both by asking about the project first and recommending after.

Send us a list of dimensions and quantities and we will discuss availability and pricing with you.
