Choosing the Right Wood

Indoor or outdoor

Wood kept dry indoors needs less protection than wood exposed to weather.

Dimensions and quantity

Thickness, width, length and count help us check availability and quote correctly.

Storage

Keep timber off the ground, covered and with air flow to reduce warping and damp.

Maintenance

Seal or polish according to use, and check regularly for moisture and insects.
