Door Styles and Designs

A door is one of the first things people notice about a home. We can help you think through main entrance doors, internal doors, and traditional or contemporary designs. Specific wood types, finishes and construction styles are confirmed by the store when you enquire.

Door Types

Main entrance doors

Stronger, more weather-exposed and usually the focal point of the front of the home.

Internal doors

For bedrooms, bathrooms and utility rooms, where budget and appearance often lead the choice.

Traditional designs

Panelled and carved looks for homes with a classic feel.

Contemporary designs

Clean, flat and minimal looks for modern interiors.

Choosing the Right Wood

Location

Entrance doors face sun and rain. Bathroom doors face damp. Say where the door will go.

Frame and size

Share the opening width, height and wall thickness so the fit is right.

Finish

Natural polish, stain or paint changes how the door looks and how it is maintained.

Budget

Give us a range and we will talk through choices within it.
